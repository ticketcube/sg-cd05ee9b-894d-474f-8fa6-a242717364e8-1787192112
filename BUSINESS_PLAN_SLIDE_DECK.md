## SLIDE 17: Risk Mitigation

**Key Risks & Mitigation:**

**Risk 1: High User Acquisition Costs**
- ✅ Mitigation: Focus on organic/viral growth, referrals
- ✅ Contingency: Reduce paid spend, extend timeline

**Risk 2: Low Ticket Conversion Rates**
- ✅ Mitigation: Enhanced personalization, exclusive pre-sales
- ✅ Contingency: Shift to advertising & merchandise focus

**Risk 3: Insufficient Advertiser Demand**
- ✅ Mitigation: Demonstrate engagement early, diversify sponsors
- ✅ Contingency: Explore subscription model

**Risk 4: Music Licensing Issues**
- ✅ Mitigation: Use YouTube/TikTok embeds (rights cleared)
- ✅ Contingency: Partner with licensing aggregators

**Risk 5: Platform Competition**
- ✅ Mitigation: Focus on differentiation, move fast
- ✅ Contingency: B2B pivot or acquisition positioning

# 📝 Presentation Notes

## Slide Design Recommendations:

**Color Scheme:**
- Primary: Music-themed purple/blue gradient
- Accent: Vibrant orange/yellow for highlights
- Background: Dark theme for modern tech feel
- Text: High contrast white on dark

**Visual Elements:**
- Use music-related icons (🎵 🎸 🎤 🎧)
- Include screenshots of actual app/MVP
- Charts for financial projections (bar/line charts)
- User flow diagrams where relevant

**Typography:**
- Headers: Bold, large (36-48pt)
- Body: Clear, readable (18-24pt)
- Use bullet points liberally (max 5-6 per slide)

**Slide Timing:**
- 25 slides = ~30-40 minute presentation
- Allow 1-2 minutes per slide
- Save 10-15 minutes for Q&A

## Presentation Tips:

1. **Start Strong:** Hook with the problem & market size
2. **Tell a Story:** Use customer journey examples
3. **Show, Don't Tell:** Demo the product if possible
4. **Address Risks:** Be transparent and show mitigation
5. **End with Clarity:** Clear ask, clear next steps

## Customization Guide:

**For Different Audiences:**

**Investors:**
- Focus on slides 11-12 (financials), 18 (investment), 23 (highlights)
- Emphasize unit economics and exit opportunities

**Partners (Labels/Artists):**
- Focus on slides 4-7 (solution & revenue), 15-16 (competitive advantage)
- Emphasize artist benefits and collaboration opportunities

**Press/Media:**
- Focus on slides 2-3 (problem/solution), 14 (go-to-market), 22 (why now)
- Emphasize innovation and market disruption

**Internal Team:**
- Focus on slides 8-10 (market & acquisition), 13 (KPIs), 14 (roadmap)
- Emphasize execution and milestones
